Chain of Custody

Cryptographically hash-chained custody ledger for evidence integrity and legal admissibility.

Hash-Chained Ledger
Each custody event is sealed into a SHA-256 hash that incorporates the hash of the event before it. Changing or reordering any past event breaks every hash after it, so tampering is mathematically detectable rather than merely logged.
